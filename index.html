General Tips

Crypts

Crypts provide materials for armor, XP, silver, food, and clan chests. It must be finished so all the clan get the chest.

The best captain is Carter.

If you can't take all the crypt at once with Carter, send Carter and another captain, so the enemy can't take the chest.

Reviving troops

Avoid reviving troops with gold; only revive mercenaries and monsters.

Clan resources

Wood → Planks, Iron → Steel, Stone → Cement, Silver → Scientific Treaties, Food → Religious Treaties.

All clan members should send resources to clan buildings. To do that, you can follow either method below:

  • From the building on the map: Click or tap the building you want to support, choose "Send resources", pick the resource and amount, then confirm.
  • From the Clan menu: Open the Clan menu (at the bottom of the screen) → Buildings → select the building → "Send resources", choose the resource and amount, then confirm.

City setup

Start on a silver tile (90%), later move to a food tile (90%). Build farms and mansions 50/50.

Captains priority

Carter, Cleopatra, Amanitore, Helen, Stror, Dustan. Wu Zetian later for G5/G6.

Ragnarök trick

Attack epics before research, then upgrade guard, then open shop for better rewards.

Dragon Mounds / Well Springs

Claim only one per reset, register in chat, arrive within 10 minutes, and do not steal claimed mounds.

Chat for Dragon Mounds : K275 DM

Chat for Well Springs : K275 DM
