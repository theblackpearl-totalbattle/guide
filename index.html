General Tips

Crypts

Crypts provide materials for armor, XP, silver, food, and clan chests. Finish them so the clan gets the chest.

The best captain is Carter. If you can't take the crypt at once with Carter, send Carter plus another captain so the enemy can't steal the chest.

Reviving troops

Avoid reviving troops with gold; only revive mercenaries and monsters when appropriate.

Clan resources

Wood → Planks, Iron → Steel, Stone → Cement, Silver → Scientific Treaties, Food → Religious Treaties.

All clan members should send resources to clan buildings. To do that:

  • From the building on the map: Click the building → "Send resources", pick resource and amount, confirm.
  • From the Clan menu: Clan → Buildings → select building → "Send resources".

City setup

Start on a silver tile (90%), later move to a food tile (90%). Build farms and mansions at a balanced ratio.

Captains priority

Carter, Cleopatra, Amanitore, Helen, Stror, Dustan. Wu Zetian later for higher-level content.

Ragnarök trick

Attack epics before research, then upgrade guard, then open shop for better rewards.

Dragon Mounds / Well Springs

Claim only one per reset, register in chat, arrive within 10 minutes, and do not steal claimed mounds.

Chat for Dragon Mounds: K275 DM — Chat for Well Springs: K275 DM
